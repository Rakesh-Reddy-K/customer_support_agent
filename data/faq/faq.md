# TechKart Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is TechKart?
TechKart is a premier online electronics retailer offering smartphones, laptops, headphones, smartwatches, and accessories from top brands at competitive prices.

### How do I create an account?
Visit techkart.com and click "Sign Up." You can register using your email address, phone number, or social media accounts (Google, Facebook).

### Is TechKart available on mobile?
Yes! Download the TechKart app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android).

## Orders & Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept:
- Credit/Debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex)
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
- Net Banking (all major banks)
- EMI options (select banks)
- Cash on Delivery (for orders under ₹50,000)
- TechKart Wallet

### Is Cash on Delivery available?
Yes, for orders under ₹50,000 in select cities. A convenience fee of ₹49 applies for COD orders.

### Can I change my payment method after placing an order?
Unfortunately, you cannot change the payment method after an order is placed. You can cancel the order and place a new one with the desired payment method (if the order hasn't shipped yet).

### Are prices inclusive of GST?
Yes, all prices displayed on TechKart include applicable GST. The GST amount will be shown in your invoice.

### Do you offer EMI?
Yes, we offer no-cost EMI on select products with partner banks. Check the product page for EMI availability.

## Products

### Are products on TechKart genuine?
Absolutely! All products are sourced directly from manufacturers or authorized distributors. We guarantee 100% authenticity.

### Do you sell refurbished products?
No, all products on TechKart are brand new unless explicitly listed under our "Certified Pre-Owned" section.

### How do I check product compatibility?
Product specifications are listed on each product page. Our AI support agent can also help you check compatibility.

### Can I compare products?
Yes, you can compare up to 4 products of the same category on our website.

## Shipping & Delivery

### How long does delivery take?
- Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days
- Express Shipping: 2-3 business days
- Same-Day Delivery: Same day (order before 2 PM, select cities)

### Do you ship PAN India?
We deliver to most locations across India. Some remote areas may have limited delivery options.

### How do I track my order?
Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email and SMS. You can also track orders in the "My Orders" section of your account.

## Returns & Refunds

### What is your return policy?
Most items can be returned within 7-15 days of delivery, depending on the product category. Products must be in original condition with all accessories.

### How do I get a refund?
Once your return is approved, the refund is processed to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

### Can I exchange a product?
Yes, exchanges are available for most products subject to availability.

## Account & Security

### How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email/phone.

### Is my payment information secure?
Yes, we use industry-standard SSL encryption and PCI DSS compliant payment processing. We never store your full card details on our servers.

## Warranty & Support

### Do products come with a warranty?
Yes, all products come with the manufacturer's warranty. Extended warranty plans are also available for purchase.

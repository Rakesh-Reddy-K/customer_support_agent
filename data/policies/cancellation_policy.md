# TechKart Cancellation Policy

## Order Cancellation

### Before Shipment
- Orders can be cancelled anytime before shipment at no charge
- Cancellation requests are processed immediately
- Refund will be initiated within 24 hours to the original payment method

### After Shipment
- Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled
- You may initiate a return once the order is delivered
- Return shipping will be at no cost if the return is within the policy window

## How to Cancel an Order

1. Log in to your TechKart account
2. Go to My Orders
3. Select the order you wish to cancel
4. Click "Cancel Order"
5. Select a reason for cancellation
6. Confirm the cancellation

## Cancellation Reasons

Common cancellation reasons:
- Changed mind / No longer needed
- Found a better price elsewhere
- Ordered by mistake
- Shipping time too long
- Payment issues

## Cancellation Timeframes

| Order Status | Can Cancel? | Refund Processing |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| PENDING | ✅ Yes | Immediate |
| CONFIRMED | ✅ Yes | Within 24 hours |
| PROCESSING | ✅ Yes (limited) | Within 24-48 hours |
| SHIPPED | ❌ No | Return after delivery |
| DELIVERED | ❌ No | Use return policy |

## Partial Cancellation

- For orders with multiple items, you can cancel individual items
- The refund will apply only to the cancelled item(s)
- Shipping charges will be recalculated if applicable

## Cancellation by TechKart

TechKart reserves the right to cancel orders in the following cases:
- Payment fraud detected
- Product out of stock after order placement
- Pricing error
- Suspected unauthorized transactions

In such cases, a full refund will be processed and you will be notified via email/SMS.

## Cancellation Limits

- Maximum 5 cancellations per customer per month
- Repeated cancellations may result in temporary account restrictions
- Bulk order cancellations require manager approval

## No-Show Cancellations

If an order is undeliverable due to customer unavailability after 3 delivery attempts:
- The order will be automatically cancelled
- A full refund will be processed
- A cancellation fee of ₹199 may apply for Same-Day Delivery orders

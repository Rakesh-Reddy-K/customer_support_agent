# TechKart Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers are eligible for a full refund if:
- The product is damaged upon delivery
- The product is defective or not working as intended
- The wrong product was delivered
- The product does not match the description on the website

## Refund Window

- **Standard products**: Refund requests must be made within **30 days** of delivery
- **Electronics**: Refund requests must be made within **15 days** of delivery
- **Accessories**: Refund requests must be made within **7 days** of delivery

## How to Request a Refund

1. Contact our AI support agent or customer service team
2. Provide your order ID and reason for the refund
3. Our team will review the request within 24-48 hours
4. If approved, refund will be processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days

## Refund Amounts

- Full refund: Product price + shipping charges (if product was defective/damaged)
- Partial refund: May apply if the product shows signs of use beyond inspection
- No refund: Processing fees are non-refundable

## Non-Refundable Items

- Products with physical damage caused by the customer
- Products with removed serial numbers or tampered seals
- Products outside the refund window
- Products purchased during special clearance sales (marked as "Final Sale")

## Refund Processing

- Refunds are processed to the original payment method
- Credit card refunds: 5-7 business days
- UPI refunds: 3-5 business days
- Net banking refunds: 5-7 business days
- Wallet refunds: 1-2 business days

## Duplicate Refund Prevention

Each order can only be refunded once. If a refund has already been processed for an order, additional refund requests for the same order will be denied.

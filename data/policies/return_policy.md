# TechKart Return Policy

## Eligibility

You may return most items within the specified return window from the date of delivery.

## Return Windows

- **Smartphones**: 15 days
- **Laptops**: 15 days
- **Headphones**: 7 days
- **Smartwatches**: 15 days
- **Accessories**: 7 days

## Conditions for Return

The product must be:
- In its original packaging
- Unused and in the same condition as received
- All accessories and documentation included
- No physical damage caused by the customer
- Original invoice/receipt available

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log in to your TechKart account
2. Go to My Orders
3. Select the item you wish to return
4. Choose a reason for the return
5. Schedule a pickup or drop off at a partner location

## Return Process

1. **Request Initiated**: You will receive a return authorization number
2. **Pickup Scheduled**: Our logistics partner will pick up the item within 2-3 business days
3. **Quality Check**: Once received, our team will inspect the product (1-2 business days)
4. **Refund/Exchange**: Based on the quality check, refund will be processed or exchange arranged

## Return Shipping

- If the return is due to a TechKart error (wrong product, damaged in transit), return shipping is free
- For all other returns, a nominal return shipping fee of ₹99 may be charged

## Exchanges

- Exchanges are subject to product availability
- If the desired replacement is not available, a full refund will be processed
- Price difference must be paid for upgrades

## Partial Returns

- For orders with multiple items, individual items can be returned separately
- The refund amount will correspond to the returned item(s) only

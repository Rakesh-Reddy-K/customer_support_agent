# TechKart Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty

All products sold on TechKart come with a manufacturer's warranty:

- **Smartphones**: 12 months
- **Laptops**: 12-36 months (varies by brand)
- **Headphones**: 12-24 months (varies by brand)
- **Smartwatches**: 12 months
- **Accessories**: 6-60 months (varies by product)

## What's Covered

- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures under normal use
- Battery issues (within the first 6 months, presumed manufacturing defect)
- Display defects
- Software issues that affect basic functionality

## What's NOT Covered

- Physical damage (drops, spills, cracked screens from customer mishandling)
- Water damage (unless product is rated water-resistant and failure is unrelated)
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Normal wear and tear
- Cosmetic damage that doesn't affect functionality
- Issues caused by using non-compatible accessories
- Damage from power surges (unless product has built-in surge protection)

## How to File a Warranty Claim

1. Contact TechKart support with your order ID
2. Describe the issue you're experiencing
3. Provide photos/videos if applicable
4. Our team will assess the claim within 24-48 hours
5. If approved, you'll receive instructions for repair/replacement

## Warranty Service Options

- **On-site service**: For laptops and large items, a technician may visit your location
- **Service center**: You may need to visit an authorized service center
- **Mail-in service**: Ship the product to our service center (prepaid shipping label provided)
- **Replacement**: If the product cannot be repaired, a replacement will be provided

## Warranty Claim Process

1. Initial assessment: 24-48 hours
2. Service/repair: 5-10 business days
3. Replacement (if needed): 7-15 business days

## Extended Warranty

TechKart offers extended warranty plans:
- **TechKart Protect Plus**: Extends warranty by 12 months
- **TechKart Protect Premium**: Extends warranty by 24 months with accidental damage coverage

Extended warranty must be purchased within 30 days of the original purchase.

## Warranty Transfer

Standard manufacturer warranties are generally not transferable. If you purchased a used product from another customer, warranty coverage may not apply to you.

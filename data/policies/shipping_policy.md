# TechKart Shipping Policy

## Shipping Methods

| Method | Delivery Time | Cost |
|--------|--------------|------|
| Standard Shipping | 5-7 business days | Free on orders above ₹999, otherwise ₹99 |
| Express Shipping | 2-3 business days | ₹199 |
| Same-Day Delivery | Same day (order before 2 PM) | ₹499 (available in select cities) |

## Shipping Coverage

We deliver to:
- All major cities across India
- Most Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities
- Some rural areas (delivery times may vary)
- Currently NOT available: Jammu & Kashmir, Northeast states (limited), and certain remote areas

## Order Processing

- Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST are processed the same business day
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST are processed the next business day
- Orders placed on weekends/holidays are processed the next business day
- Payment verification may take additional time for some payment methods

## Tracking

- Once shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email and SMS
- Track your order at: https://track.techkart.com
- Real-time tracking updates are available for Express and Same-Day deliveries
- Standard shipping tracking updates every 24 hours

## Shipping Partners

- TechKart Express (our logistics arm)
- Delhivery
- BlueDart
- FedEx
- India Post (for remote areas)

## Delivery Instructions

- Please ensure someone is available to receive the package
- A valid ID proof may be required for high-value deliveries (above ₹50,000)
- For apartments/buildings, please provide complete address with flat number and building name

## Failed Deliveries

- If delivery fails, our partner will attempt redelivery within 2 business days
- After 3 failed attempts, the package will be returned to our warehouse
- A full refund will be processed for returned packages

## Damaged Shipments

If your package arrives damaged:
1. Take photos of the damaged packaging and product
2. Contact our support team immediately
3. Do not discard the packaging until instructed
4. We will arrange a pickup and send a replacement or process a refund

## Special Items

- Laptops and smartphones are shipped with additional protective packaging
- Fragile items include extra cushioning and handling labels
- High-value items require signature on delivery
